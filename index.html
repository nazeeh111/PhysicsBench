      THE FIELD GUIDE

      Start with a question.

      Five instruments. Three ways to investigate.

      01 / Simulate

      Pick a mechanics lab, try a preset, or change the SI inputs and apply. Scrub exact time, explore a parameter sweep, then send the trajectory to analysis. Model assumptions are below each experiment.

      02 / Measure

      Sound laboratory starts with a silent synthetic signal. Enable your microphone only when ready. Motion & data accepts a local video for manual point tracking or a two-column time,value CSV.

      03 / Make a case

      Fit a line or parabola, inspect residuals, and export evidence. A good fit alone does not validate a physical model. Video needs a known length and a suitable camera angle before reporting metres.

      Your machine, your data.

      No accounts, uploads, analytics, AI services or external fonts. Simulations and demos need no hardware permission. Microphone capture needs HTTPS or localhost, a supported browser, available hardware and your permission. Local-video formats depend on your browser codecs.

      Use a current Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari browser on Windows, macOS, Linux or ChromeOS. No dedicated GPU required. Phone-only sensors such as magnetometers are not assumed. Actual sound level in dB SPL is not available without calibration.

      Use Tab to reach controls, Enter to activate, Escape to close this guide and Ctrl/⌘ K to find an instrument. Reduced motion replaces mechanics playback with single steps.
