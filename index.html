      LOCAL EXPERIMENT RECORDS

      Saved investigations

      Records retain source data and settings. Reopening recomputes the analysis. For video files, reattach the original clip when requested.

      Compare exactly two named conditions, with at least two independent acquisitions in each. Re-saving one recording does not create another repetition. Keep geometry, gain and processing fixed.

      METHODS & LIMITS

      An investigation you can question.

      Acoustic identification

      The known sweep learns the finite signal-path model and latency. An independent random probe tests its prediction. No probe response is used to choose gain, alignment or scoring window. Compare against the simpler delay-and-gain baseline.

      The response includes speakers, microphone, room and capture processing. It is not calibrated SPL or an absolute distance measurement. Silence, clipping and mismatched predictions can withhold a result.

      Motion inference

      Track a visible object, inspect failed matches, correct or exclude points, calibrate scale, and compare a linear or ballistic model. Generated specimens identify their source. Video precision, geometry and tracking errors limit the result.

      A polynomial fit alone does not validate gravity. Review the data window and residuals. A source video must be reattached after reopening its record; observations remain available.

      Reproducibility

      Save local runs to keep observations and settings across reloads. Export a complete record to move it between browsers. Reopening recomputes derived results rather than trusting cached scores.

      IndexedDB availability and quota vary; storage failures are reported. Browser data clearing removes locally saved runs, so export important work.

      Reference specimens and virtual acquisitions require no device permission. Real acoustic capture requests microphone access and explicitly emits a quiet test signal. The app never automatically monitors microphone input through speakers.

      Current browser APIs and optional hardware capabilities vary. The permission-free investigations remain available. Reduced motion disables continuous mechanics playback. Use native keyboard controls, Tab and Escape.
